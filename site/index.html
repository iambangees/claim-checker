Claim Checker

Before you claim, check what it really costs. A "free" airdrop, whitelist or lending offer can quietly ask for your social account, your wallet's security settings, a signature — or real money. Run the pitch through here first.

1 · Paste the pitch

Paste the post, message or offer text you were sent. The checker scans it for trap phrases and ticks the matching boxes below — you stay in control and can untick anything.

2 · The red-flag checklist

Tick everything the offer asks of you or admits about itself. Be honest — ticking a box here costs nothing; missing one out there can.

The golden rules

  1. A claim that costs money is not free. Gas, mint fees, "activation" payments and minimum buys are a price — decide if the reward is worth it before paying.
  2. Check the official site first. If the product (a lending app, a bonus, a second mint round) is not mentioned on the project's own website or verified channels, treat it as unverified — lookalike bots and apps are a standard phishing pattern.
  3. What you can't transfer, you can't sell. A non-transferable NFT or a locked token has no exit. "Claim now, sell later" only works if selling is actually possible.
  4. Your post is a payment. If claiming requires posting promotion on your own X or social account, you are paying with your reputation and your audience. Price it in.
  5. Security settings are a cost too. Developer Mode, broad approvals and wallet connections widen what an operator can do. Know exactly what access you are granting, and to whom.
  6. Never type a seed phrase or private key anywhere. No legitimate claim needs it. Ever. The same goes for pairing codes — a real project never asks for yours.

Claim Checker runs entirely in your browser — nothing you paste is uploaded anywhere. It is a checklist, not a guarantee: a clean result means no known traps were found, not that an offer is safe.
